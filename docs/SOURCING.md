# Sourcing policy

Every fact on this site is in exactly one of three states. The state is visible
to the reader, always.

| State | Means | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | Traced to a primary document, with URL and retrieval date | Teal badge |
| `attributed` | Someone else's claim — quoted, named and linked, never paraphrased | Named inline |
| `unsourced` | We looked and found nothing | Amber outline, left visibly empty |

## Rules

1. **Never infer a candidate's position.** Not from party history, donors, a past
   job, or what similar candidates usually think. No position without a quote we
   can link.
2. **An empty slot is published as empty.** A silent candidate is a finding.
3. **No endorsements, scores or grades.** Grading candidates would arguably make
   this third-party advertising under the Municipal Elections Act (see RISKS.md).
4. **Right of reply, before and after.** Every candidate is emailed their page.
   Corrections are logged below with a date.
5. **Criticism is quoted and attributed**, never absorbed into our voice.
6. **Never machine-translate a quote and present it as someone's words.** Quotes
   stay in their original language with a clearly-labelled translation beneath.
